E1 · A cook mode you can read from two feet away

Split the recipe into one step per screen, with the screen kept awake.

"I keep losing my place and my hands are covered in flour." — you, last session

Recommend it, and recommend it first. Today the whole recipe is one scrolling page at body-text size: finding your place after a scroll means re-reading, and the screen dims mid-simmer. One step per screen at 15pt solves both, and it's additive — the existing recipe view stays exactly as it is for reading on the couch.

  • Step-per-screen, not a bigger font. Enlarging the existing list would push a four-step recipe to three screens of scrolling. The problem isn't size alone, it's that nothing marks where you are.
  • Ingredients repeat on every step. Only the ones that step uses. This is the single thing that stops the back-and-forth to the top of the page.
  • Keep-awake is scoped to cook mode. Entering sets it, leaving clears it, and a banner says so — a screen that silently never sleeps is a battery bug the user can't diagnose.
  • Deviation flag. DESIGN.md says every screen is reachable from the tab bar. Cook mode isn't — it's modal, entered from a recipe and exited back to it. Flagging it rather than quietly breaking the rule.

E2 · Stop sorting the list by the day you saved it

Group the recipe list by course, keep newest-first inside each group.

"By the time I have 40 of these I'll never find the soup." — you, two sessions ago

Recommend it, with one reservation. Save-date ordering is only useful for the handful you added this week; past about twenty recipes it becomes a scroll hunt. Course grouping matches how you actually decide ("what am I making for dinner"). The reservation: it needs a course on every recipe, and the eleven already saved don't have one.

  • Course, not cuisine or tag. Cuisine splits too fine on a small library — you'd get seven groups of one. Course gives four or five real groups.
  • Recently saved stays reachable. It becomes a segmented filter at the top rather than the default order, so nothing that works today gets worse.
  • The eleven existing recipes need backfilling. Proposal: default them all to "Mains" and let you re-file from the row. No migration, no schema change — the field already exists and is nullable.
  • Open question, genuinely. Should an unfiled recipe get its own "Unsorted" group at the top, or silently sit in Mains? The wireframe shows Unsorted.
